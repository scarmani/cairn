## What the saved proofs establish

Every completed producer exhausted its 32-node limit and left root bounds
`[-1, 1]`. Every completed checker returned `unknown/node-limit`, unverified;
none supplied a complete optimal-action set. The six legacy rulesets additionally
lack the laboratory oracle's independent-mechanics path. Successful callback
completion is therefore neither full certification nor admission.

The read-only audit validated all six completed result records and their 24
unique content-addressed artifact references, including task, origin, source,
provider, certificate, and checked-report bindings. It detected no integrity
contradiction. Forty-one content-addressed files exist: seventeen are uncompleted
stage artifacts and do not confer completed-task status. Some record independent
producer work; they are not complete dual-provider results or latency samples.

The six completed attempts took 8.561–12.347 seconds each. Recorded producer
times were 2.076–3.005 seconds and checker times 6.050–8.830 seconds. Checking
accounted for 74.43% of their combined recorded producer/checker time. This
identifies a cost center in this small workload, not a performance estimate for
other rules, sizes, node budgets, or complete proofs. No p95 is claimed.

## Gate disposition

There is no completed dual-provider timing sample, no Beginner sample, and no
measured full-certification scaling. Linear extrapolation from six heterogeneous
legacy tasks cannot fill those gaps. Therefore a subsequent full-certification
cohort has no defensible measured projection under the frozen launch gate.

Further charged certification, MCTS admission, and comparative matches are
blocked. The 36-hour allowance is not exhausted; it is not permission to waive a
failed feasibility gate. No automatic retry, longer timeout, replacement task,
fabricated history, alternative search architecture, or native-only comparative
fallback was launched. A further charged continuation needs an explicitly
predeclared measurement plan. Existing negative artifacts and the single budget
ledger must remain intact; any permitted resume also needs explicit supervisor
reconciliation, even though no foreign active ledger lease remains.

This says nothing adverse about game correctness, termination, strategic depth,
or beauty. Batch6 can still produce evidence cards, mechanical motif tooling,
and a clearly separate provisional design-priority discussion. It cannot fill
the missing comparative or human-observation evidence.
